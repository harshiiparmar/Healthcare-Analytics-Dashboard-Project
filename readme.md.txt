Healthcare Vision Analytics Dashboard
Project Overview
The Healthcare Vision Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze healthcare data and provide meaningful insights into patient admissions, medical conditions, hospital performance, and revenue trends.
The objective of this project is to transform raw healthcare data into actionable business insights that can help healthcare organizations improve operational efficiency, resource planning, and patient care.

 Dashboard Preview

 Business Problem
Healthcare organizations generate large volumes of data every day. Without proper analysis, it becomes difficult to identify patterns, monitor performance, and make informed decisions.
This dashboard helps answer key questions such as:
* How many patients were admitted?
* What are the most common medical conditions?
* Which admission types are most frequent?
* How much revenue is generated?
* What trends exist in patient admissions?

 Key Insights
1. Patient Admissions Continue to Grow
The dashboard shows a steady increase in patient admissions throughout the year, indicating growing healthcare demand.
2. Balanced Admission Distribution
Elective, Urgent, and Emergency admissions are distributed relatively evenly, demonstrating a balanced patient intake across admission categories.
3. Chronic Diseases Dominate Patient Volume
The most common medical conditions include:
* Arthritis
* Diabetes
* Hypertension
* Obesity
These conditions account for a significant portion of total patients.
4. Revenue Driven by Chronic Conditions
Diabetes and Obesity contribute heavily to healthcare revenue, highlighting the financial impact of long-term disease management.
5. Seasonal Admission Patterns
Admission trends suggest periods of increased healthcare demand that may require additional staffing and resources.

 Recommendations
Preventive Healthcare Programs
Develop awareness and prevention programs targeting chronic diseases such as Diabetes and Hypertension.
Resource Planning
Allocate healthcare resources more effectively during high-admission periods.
Predictive Analytics
Implement forecasting models to predict future patient admissions and resource requirements.
Telemedicine Expansion
Increase telehealth services to improve accessibility and reduce hospital congestion.
Chronic Disease Monitoring
Strengthen long-term patient monitoring programs to improve outcomes and reduce readmissions.
